**Tags:** `[famous]` = widely attributed / public domain. `[original]` = written for Baby Facts Unlocked.

**Selector rules:**
- Pick an unused quote (not in `used.md`)
- Bias toward `[famous]` 70% of the time (algorithm-weighted)
- After posting, append `Q### | YYYY-MM-DD | platform` to `used.md`

**Review:** Strike through or delete any quote you don't want to post. Add new ones at the bottom of the relevant theme with the next available ID.
